Each tier is unlocked by one research project. They all sit in their own research tab, **All-Bamba Tech**, which the author's other "For All" mods share.

The order depends on what else you have loaded:

- **The mod alone:** Industrial, then Spacer, then Ultra.
- **With Beyond Our Reach:** Industrial, Spacer, <span class="t-solar">Solar</span>, Ultra, <span class="t-kwazaari">Kwazaari</span>. Ultra comes after Solar, so the tiers climb in the same order as Beyond Our Reach's own technology. Each BOR tier also needs that mod's matching weapons research. <span class="t-tenebral">Tenebral</span> weapons have no research; see [With Beyond Our Reach](beyond-our-reach.html).
- **With Combat Extended, without Beyond Our Reach:** Industrial and Spacer only. Ultra gear can't be crafted, so its research is removed.
- **With both:** Ultra research costs far more and has to be done at a hi-tech research bench with a multi-analyzer.
