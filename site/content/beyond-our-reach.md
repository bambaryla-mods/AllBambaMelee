[Beyond Our Reach :: Redux](https://databobek.github.io/RimworldBeyondOurReach/) extends the late game with new tiers of technology. This mod was built alongside it, and with both loaded you get a full melee set in three more tiers. Everything on this page needs Beyond Our Reach; without it none of these weapons exist.

{{ tier_icons("solar", "kwazaari", "tenebral") }}

## The three tiers

**<span class="t-solar">Solar</span>** weapons are forged from sunsteel and sunweave at Beyond Our Reach's sunsteel shaper, or any of its later fabricators. They need the *solar tier melee weapons* research, which itself needs BOR's *solar weaponry*. Solar sits between Spacer and Ultra.

**<span class="t-kwazaari">Kwazaari</span>** weapons are made from Kwazaari alloy, weave and universal parts at the Kwazifab or the umbral fabricator. They need *kwazaari tier melee weapons*, which comes after Ultra and BOR's *kwazaari weaponry*.

**<span class="t-tenebral">Tenebral</span>** weapons are made of tenebrite crystals and crystalline joints at the umbral fabricator, with no research of their own. Beyond Our Reach's dark archotech altar offers the whole set as well, shield included with Combat Extended, but only a pawn in tenebral psychosis can work it. The Tenebral shield comes only from the altar. The [Tenebral road](https://databobek.github.io/RimworldBeyondOurReach/tenebral-road.html) page on the BOR site explains how to get there.

The Solar and Kwazaari melee research sits in Beyond Our Reach's research tab, not in All-Bamba Tech; see [Research](research.html). The stats, recipes and research for all three tiers are on the [Weapons](weapons.html), [Shields](shields.html) and [Research](research.html) pages, next to the other tiers.

## In Beyond Our Reach's factions

With this mod loaded, Beyond Our Reach's factions bring melee fighters armed with these weapons:

- The Solar Republic's raids and settlements include melee troops.
- Tenebral raids include melee fighters.
- The Kwazaari send melee members in their reinforcement squads, and add a new favor: an anti-mech squad of four mechbreakers armed with EMP maces.

See the [Beyond Our Reach site](https://databobek.github.io/RimworldBeyondOurReach/bamba-integrations.html) for the full list of what each of the author's mods adds there.
