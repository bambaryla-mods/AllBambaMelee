## What the mod does

Vanilla RimWorld gives melee fighters a few medieval weapons and then a big jump to persona and plasma weapons. This mod fills the gap with eight weapon families, each made in several tiers:

- **Knife, gladius and longsword** for cutting and stabbing.
- **Hammer** (an **axe** in the Spacer and Ultra tiers) and **polehammer** for crushing armor.
- **Mace**, which adds an EMP jolt to stun mechanoids.
- **Baton**, a less-than-lethal stick that stuns.
- **Spear**, with extra reach if Vanilla Expanded Framework is loaded.

The tiers are <span class="t-industrial">Industrial</span>, <span class="t-spacer">Spacer</span> and <span class="t-ultra">Ultra</span>. With [Beyond Our Reach](beyond-our-reach.html) loaded you also get <span class="t-solar">Solar</span>, <span class="t-kwazaari">Kwazaari</span> and <span class="t-tenebral">Tenebral</span> weapons. Each tier has its own research in the **All-Bamba Tech** research tab, and every weapon is made at a bench from that tier's materials.

With Combat Extended loaded, every tier also gets a shield, and all weapons get CE stats. The stat pages here have a switch at the top to show either version.

## What you need

Nothing besides the game. The mod runs on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 and needs no other mods. Two are optional and fully supported:

- **Beyond Our Reach :: Redux** adds the Solar, Kwazaari and Tenebral tiers.
- **Combat Extended** adds the shields and switches every weapon to CE stats.

Load this mod after both of them.
