Each tier has the same eight weapon families, so you can keep the weapon type your fighters like and just upgrade it. Higher tiers hit harder, punch through more armor and cost rarer materials.

| Weapon | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Knife | Light and fast. A sidearm for anyone, not just melee fighters. |
| Gladius | A short sword. Stabs well and stays light. |
| Longsword | The heaviest cutting damage in each tier. |
| Hammer / axe | Heavy blows. Spacer and Ultra make an axe instead of a hammer. |
| Polehammer | A hammer on a long shaft, to crush armor inwards. |
| Mace | Blunt, with extra EMP damage that stuns mechanoids. |
| Baton | Less-than-lethal, with extra stun damage. Good for taking prisoners. |
| Spear | Long reach with Vanilla Expanded Framework loaded. |

Quality scales damage like on any vanilla weapon. With Combat Extended, the longsword, polehammer and spear are two-handed and can't be used with a shield. Hover over a weapon's name for its in-game description. Under the name you'll find the research it needs, the benches that make it, and the ingredients.

## Where to get them

You craft them. Industrial weapons are made at a smithy, Spacer weapons at an electric smithy or fabrication bench, and Ultra weapons at a fabrication bench. The Beyond Our Reach tiers need that mod's benches; see [With Beyond Our Reach](beyond-our-reach.html).

With Combat Extended but without Beyond Our Reach, Ultra weapons can't be crafted. The Empire's elite melee fighters carry them, exotic, combat and Empire traders stock them, and they turn up as quest rewards. With both mods loaded they can be crafted again, but the research is much more expensive; see [Research](research.html).
