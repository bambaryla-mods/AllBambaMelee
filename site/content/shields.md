Shields only exist with **Combat Extended** loaded, because they use CE's shield system. Without CE this page doesn't apply to your game.

A shield is worn like apparel and blocks hits on the body parts it covers. A crouching pawn hides their legs behind it too. Your fighter can only carry a one-handed weapon alongside it: the knife, gladius, hammer, axe, mace or baton. Longswords, polehammers and spears need both hands.

A shield gets in the way of everything else. Shooting and aiming suffer, melee hits land less often and crit less, and reloading slows down. In return the wearer gets a much better chance to parry melee attacks and is much harder to suppress. Higher tiers give more armor and take less away.
